雷达发展史-I “现代”雷达诞生，1928～1940

现代雷达和 微波之父

1934，Hollmann built the first radar for detecting ships. The radar operated on 50 cm wave length and could find ships up to 10 km away. 1935, a pulse radar was developed with which they could spot the ship the “Koenigsberg” 8 km away. For ship born application, they used the same radar system, except that the ship born system operated on a wave length of 80 cm instead of 1.8-2.0 m. The land based radar was called "Freya" and the sea born radar was called "Seetakt."
